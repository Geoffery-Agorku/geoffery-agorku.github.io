About

Applied transportation research with national infrastructure relevance

My research focuses on inland waterway and multimodal freight systems, with methods spanning machine learning, computer vision, AIS-based vessel analytics, and geospatial modeling.

Research profile

I am a Ph.D. student in Civil Engineering (Transportation Engineering) and a Doctoral Academy Fellow at the University of Arkansas. My work develops operational tools for infrastructure planning, freight network analysis, and transportation monitoring.

I have collaborated with the U.S. Army Engineer Research and Development Center and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers on applied analytics for national freight systems, including multimodal routing, commodity-flow visualization, and river information systems.
